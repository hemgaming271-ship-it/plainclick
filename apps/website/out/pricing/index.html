What “lifetime” means here

  • Your licence for PlainClick v1 never expires and has no subscription.
  • It includes every v1.x update. A future v2, if there is one, would be optional and sold separately.
  • It covers up to two Macs you own. Move it by deactivating it on the old Mac. If a Mac is lost or broken, email support and we'll free its slot.
  • The first activation needs an internet connection. After that the panel works offline.
  • It is not a promise that v1 will keep working with every future macOS release, and it doesn't include unlimited custom development or support forever.

Offline use and refunds

Because an activated copy works offline, a refunded copy can't be switched off straight away while that Mac stays offline. The licence is revoked on our side, and the app stops being licensed the next time it checks in.

If PlainClick ever closes

Copies that are already activated keep working, because the licence check is stored on your Mac. Before shutting down, we would publish a final build or instructions that don't need our activation server.
