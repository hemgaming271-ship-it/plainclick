Help & FAQ

Free guide: make macOS easier to read

These are Apple's own settings. They're free, they need nothing from PlainClick, and they apply to the whole system, not to one app.

  1. Open the Apple menu (top-left corner) →System Settings.
  2. Choose Accessibility in the sidebar, then Display.
  3. Turn on Reduce transparency to replace translucent backgrounds with solid ones.
  4. Turn on Increase contrast to make borders and controls stand out more.

Wording and placement can vary between macOS versions. If you can't find a setting, type its name into the search field at the top of System Settings. To undo, switch the same settings off.

Frequently asked questions

Does PlainClick change how my apps look?

No. It adds a separate panel. The app's toolbar and buttons stay exactly as they are.

Which apps work?

Only the combinations on the compatibility page. Other apps show a “not supported” message.

Why does it need Accessibility access?

macOS only lets one app press another app's commands with this permission. PlainClick uses it to find and press the supported menu commands, on your Mac only. To turn it off, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.

Does it send anything about what I'm doing to you?

No. Screen contents, window and document names, document text and browsing history never leave your Mac. Licensing uses a small amount of data, described in the privacy policy.

A button is greyed out.

The app reports that command as unavailable right now, so PlainClick doesn't send it.

Why aren't Back and Forward included?

In our tests, macOS often reported Back and Forward as unavailable when they actually were available, and pressing them didn't work reliably. We only offer actions that work reliably.

How do I move my licence to a new Mac?

On the old Mac, open PlainClick → Settings → Licence → Deactivate this Mac, then activate on the new one. If the old Mac is lost or broken, email support from your purchase address and we'll free its slot (see Find your licence key).

macOS says it can't verify PlainClick.

Current builds aren't notarized by Apple yet. Allow PlainClick once in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. The download page has the steps. Never turn off Gatekeeper.

How is the trial limited?

The 7-day trial is tracked on your Mac. It isn't tamper-proof, and we chose not to add invasive copy protection. If PlainClick is useful to you, please buy a licence.

How do I uninstall?

  1. Choose Quit from the PlainClick menu-bar icon.
  2. Move PlainClick from Applications to the Bin.
  3. Remove PlainClick from System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.
  4. Optionally delete ~/Library/Preferences/ files whose names start with the PlainClick bundle identifier.

Still stuck?

Email mgcounsultancy@gmail.com. Include your macOS version and the app you were using, but never your licence key.
