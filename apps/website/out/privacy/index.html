Privacy policy

PlainClick does its work on your Mac. Selling and licensing it needs a small amount of data, described here.

Draft. This text is a working draft prepared before launch and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Nothing here limits rights you have under the consumer law where you live.

On your Mac

  • PlainClick reads the menu commands of supported apps through macOS Accessibility, so it can press them.
  • It does not upload screen contents, accessibility trees, window or document names, document text, or browsing history.
  • Your settings and pinned actions are stored locally. Your licence is stored in your Mac's Keychain.
  • There are no analytics and no automatic crash uploads. If you choose to send a diagnostic report, you'll see what it contains first, with personal details removed.

When you buy

Payments are handled by Dodo Payments, which acts as the reseller (merchant of record). It collects your payment details, billing country and email, and we never see your card details. We can see your email, the order ID, price, currency and refund status in its dashboard, so we can support your licence.

When you activate

The app sends your licence key and your Mac's model identifier (like “Mac14,2”, not your computer's name or serial number) to Dodo Payments, which issues the keys and enforces the two-Mac limit. When online, the app occasionally asks Dodo whether the key is still valid on this Mac.

Website

  • No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and no analytics.
  • This site is static: it has no forms and stores nothing about you. The host may keep standard access logs.

Service providers

Dodo Payments (payments, licence keys and receipt emails), GitHub (download hosting) and our website host. Each processes data only to provide its service.

Keeping and deleting data

Purchase records are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires. You can ask us to remove activation records at any time.
