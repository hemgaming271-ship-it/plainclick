Find your licence key

Lost your key, or need to free up a Mac you no longer have?

Your key is in your purchase email

Our payment provider, Dodo Payments, emails your licence key right after you pay. Search your inbox (and spam folder) for Dodo Payments or PlainClick, using the email address you entered at checkout.

Still can't find it, or a Mac is lost or broken?

Email mgcounsultancy@gmail.com from the address you bought with, and include the order or payment ID from your receipt if you have it. We'll resend your key or free the lost Mac's activation. Never send us your card details.

Moving to a new Mac

On the old Mac, open PlainClick › Settings › Licence › Deactivate this Mac, then paste the key on the new one.
